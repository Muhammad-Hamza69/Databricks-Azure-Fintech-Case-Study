# Vivan E-commerce Data Platform

Welcome to the Vivan project wiki. This case study explains how decentralized e-commerce data is brought into a centralized Azure Databricks lakehouse and used for analytics, Salesforce visitor engagement, and purchase-propensity modeling.

**Start here: [Case Study Blog](Case-Study-Blog)**

The case study covers:

- Vivan's business context and the problems created by separate customer and product datasets.
- Azure Functions, Event Hubs, ADLS Gen2, Databricks Auto Loader, and Unity Catalog.
- dbt transformations, data-quality checks, and Gold analytics marts.
- Visitor-item feature engineering, ten-model comparison, and isotonic calibration.
- Streamlit scoring, Salesforce activation, recorded outcomes, and future improvements.

![Vivan architecture](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Muhammad-Hamza69/Databricks-Azure-Fintech-Case-Study/main/ululul.png)

The implementation uses public RetailRocket data and synthetic events. Historical technical results are linked to repository evidence in the case study.
